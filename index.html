Dedicated Mission

Founded by Rohingya Women for Women Across Myanmar.

Established on November 19, 2026, in Yangon, NexGen Sisterhood (NGS) was formed to provide direct, impactful solutions for women and girls. We operate as an independent non-governmental organization focused purely on women's rights, healthcare access, safety, education, and economic autonomy.

Our holistic strategy ensures that every participant receives support across physical health, emergency protection, technical training, digital security, and active participation in community decision-making processes.

Our Vision

A peaceful society where women across Myanmar are healthy, educated, protected from harm, and exercise full decision-making authority.

Our Commitment

Directly implementing 10 focus areas across maternal care, GBV protection, reproductive rights, schooling, and technical empowerment.

Our 10 Core Focus Areas

Comprehensive Women's Development

Every single pillar represents a dedicated, active programmatic initiative delivered by NGS for women across Myanmar.

Pillar 1

Maternal & Newborn Care

Essential prenatal health packages, skilled midwife referral networks, newborn nutrition kits, and emergency transport assistance for expectant mothers.

Pillar 2

Protection from Gender-Based Violence

Confidential 24/7 emergency response, survivor case management, safe shelter referrals, legal aid navigation, and community protection networks.

Pillar 3

Sexual & Reproductive Health Services

Confidential family planning advice, reproductive wellness screenings, health education circles, and access to female healthcare workers.

Pillar 4

Menstrual Hygiene & Sanitation

Dignity hygiene kit distribution, reusable pad tailoring workshops, school sanitation upgrades, and stigma-reduction community education.

Pillar 5

Mental Health Support

Trauma-informed counseling, confidential peer support circles, stress management, and community-led psychosocial healing spaces for women.

Pillar 6

Vocational & Technical Training

Garment sewing, machine maintenance, artisanal craft production, micro-enterprise accounting, and business seed toolkits.

Pillar 7

Uninterrupted Schooling

Scholarship stipends for young girls, catch-up learning centers, home study kits, literacy classes, and educational drop-out prevention.

Pillar 8

Digital Literacy & Online Safety

Practical mobile device literacy, online safety toolkits, cyberbullying defense, digital privacy, and online business communication.

Pillar 9

Decision-Making Power

Public administration workshops, advocacy leadership, community dialogue facilitation, negotiation training, and representation in local councils.

Pillar 10

Safe Public Spaces

Community safety mapping, well-lit women-only resource havens, gender-responsive public transportation advocacy, and safe market access.
